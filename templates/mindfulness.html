Progressive Muscle Body Scan

Somatic Reset

Systematically release stored tension across key muscle groups.

1. Facial & Jaw Release
Unclench your jaw, soften your tongue, and let your brow smooth out completely.
2. Shoulder Drop
Inhale deeply and gently draw your shoulders up to your ears; exhale and let them drop down completely.
3. Diaphragm Softening
Allow your abdomen to expand naturally with each breath without holding your stomach in.
4. Grounding Feet
Feel the firm pressure of both feet resting flat on the floor, releasing leg tension into the ground.

Evidence-Based Micro-Wellness Tips

Morning Sunlight Exposure
10–15 minutes of outdoor daylight within an hour of waking stabilizes the circadian clock and elevates daytime alertness.
Post-Work Cognitive Closure
Write down tomorrow's top 3 tasks before ending your workday. This psychological ritual prevents work thoughts from intruding into evening recovery.
Physiological Sigh
Take two quick inhales through the nose, followed by one long, slow exhale through the mouth to rapidly discharge acute sympathetic stress.
